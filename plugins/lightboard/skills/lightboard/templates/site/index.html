House rules

  • Each exercise says how long it takes and what you will notice when it worked. If you do not notice it, it did not work: go again.
  • Every lesson ends with an Encore: a short game, played rather than prepared. Do it even when you skip the rest.
  • Some figures build step by step as you scroll. Show all steps above each one turns that off.
