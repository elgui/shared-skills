# Intake

**D** = deduced, **A** = answered. This is the skill's demo, so the "learner" is whoever builds
the next course with the skill.

## The learner
- Who: a session (or a person) about to build a course with Lightboard. **D**
- Knows cold: HTML, CSS, the idea of scroll-driven pages. Never explain those. **D**
- Missing: when motion earns its place, and what a lesson must contain. **D**

## The subject and the gear
- Subject: the Lightboard blocks (walkthrough, compare, flow, rooms, encore). **D**
- Gear: the runtime in `runtime/`, version 1.0.0; a phone and a desktop browser. **D**

## The two rooms
- Draft: planning on paper; an exercise ends with something **written**. **D**
- Try: using the page; an exercise ends with something **seen**. **D**

## Their own material to fold in
- None; the skill's own documents are the sources. **D**

## Shape
- Two lessons, one phase; unit "Lesson"; English; local only (not published). **D**
