House rules

  • Each exercise says how long it takes and what you will notice when it worked.
  • Every lesson ends with an Encore: a short game, played rather than prepared.
  • Some figures build step by step as you scroll. Show all steps above each one turns that off, on every page, on this device.
  • Dotted words are reminders: flat mode.
  • Every claim links its source. A check it yourself flag means no source confirmed it.
